## Overview & Motivation

Natural language generation systems often struggle with computational humor because humor hinges on surprise, phonological wordplay, and cultural timing. The challenge amplifies drastically when generations must satisfy hard constraints—such as incorporating mandatory target lexical items while strictly remaining within predefined thematic topics across multiple languages.

In **SemEval-2026 Task 1**, our team (**CUETClashing**) tackled multilingual joke generation subjected to rigorous lexical and topical boundaries using small instruction-tuned LLMs.

## Technical Highlights

- **Small Instruction-Tuned LLMs:** Explored efficient, open small language models (SLMs) to investigate whether high-constraint creative generation can be achieved without relying on massive closed commercial APIs.
- **Controlled Generation Pipelines:** Formulated constrained decoding strategies and iterative refinement prompts to guarantee 100% lexical constraint satisfaction while maximizing humor plausibility.
- **Multilingual Evaluation:** Assessed generation quality across diverse languages, analyzing cross-cultural semantic fidelity and syntactic coherence.
