## Overview & Motivation

Internet memes represent a complex, evolving medium of multimodal communication where neither text nor image in isolation conveys the true underlying message. Misogynistic and abusive rhetoric frequently hides behind ironic visual juxtapositions, culturally situated metaphors, and visual satire, making traditional unimodal NLP or computer vision classifiers vulnerable to severe false negatives.

In this work, our team (**CUET_320**) tackled the **LT-EDI@LDK 2025 International Shared Task**, dedicated to identifying misogynistic content in Chinese internet memes. 

## Key Technical Contributions

- **Cross-Modal Alignment & Dual-Stream Encoding:** Formulated a deep multi-stream architecture combining visual encoders with multilingual language models fine-tuned to capture subtle contextual nuance across colloquial Mandarin online slang.
- **Fusion Mechanism:** Engineered cross-attention and gating mechanisms allowing the model to attend to dissonant image-text signals where benign surface text masked aggressive visual stereotypes.
- **Robust Evaluation:** Outperformed competing international submissions across macro F1-score and accuracy, clinching **1st place globally** in the competition.
