## Research Summary

With the proliferation of multilingual generative LLMs, automated social engineering and deceptive SMS (smishing) have emerged as significant cybersecurity threats, particularly in mobile-first developing economies where Bengali is the primary medium of communication.

## Scope & Contribution

- **Corpus Development:** Collecting real-world fraudulent Bangla SMS alongside controlled synthetic counterparts generated using diverse prompting strategies across modern LLMs.
- **Computational Stylometry:** Quantifying syntactic complexity, lexical richness, punctuation cadence, and emotional valence differences between human scam authors and generative models.
- **Empirical Human Evaluation:** Conducting human subject evaluations to assess the vulnerability of native Bengali speakers to synthetic vs. authentic deceptive messages.
