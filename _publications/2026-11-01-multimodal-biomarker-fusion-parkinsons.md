## Research Summary

Parkinson’s Disease (PD) manifests insidiously, with subtle motor and non-motor vocal symptoms often appearing years prior to formal clinical staging. Conventional single-modality automated diagnostics (e.g., voice acoustic analysis alone or gait sensor signals alone) lack diagnostic robustness across heterogeneous patient cohorts.

## Scope & Contribution

- **Heterogeneous Multimodal Fusion:** Designing cross-modality attention encoders to model heterogeneous biomedical signals and clinical attributes.
- **Transformer-Based Multimodal Integration:** Employing cross-attention fusion layers to capture synergistic biomarker interactions across modalities.
- **Explainability & Clinical Actionability:** Applying SHAP (Shapley Additive exPlanations) and Grad-CAM feature attribution to reveal the clinical features driving model predictions, establishing transparency required for clinical adoption.
