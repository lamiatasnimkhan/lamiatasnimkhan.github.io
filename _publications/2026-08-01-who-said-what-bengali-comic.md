## Research Summary

Sequential visual art and comics present unique challenges for multimodal models because textual speech bubbles must be mapped to visual speakers across irregular spatial panel arrangements, non-linear reading orders, and stylistic visual cues. While benchmarks exist for English and Japanese manga, low-resource regional languages such as Bengali have had zero standardized benchmarks for multimodal comic conversation understanding.

## Scope & Contribution

- **Novel Benchmark Dataset:** Curating an extensive, ethically sourced dataset of Bengali comic panels paired with structured question-answer pairs, character dialogue attributions, and reading path annotations.
- **Multimodal Evaluation:** Benchmarking state-of-the-art proprietary (GPT-4o, Claude 3.5 Sonnet, Gemini 1.5 Pro) and open-weight Vision-Language Models (LLaVA, Qwen2-VL) on panel-to-dialogue grounding.
- **Error Taxonomy:** Introducing a systematic taxonomy of vision-language failures on non-Western cultural scripts and non-linear narrative layouts.
