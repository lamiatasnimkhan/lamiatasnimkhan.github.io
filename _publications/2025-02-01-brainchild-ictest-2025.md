## Overview & System Architecture

Fair and transparent democratic participation depends on secure, accessible, and automated electoral workflows. This paper presents **Brainchild**, a comprehensive smart mobile architecture engineered to minimize voter friction and mitigate fraudulent activity during electoral cycles.

## Core Contributions

- **ML-Based Fraud Detection:** Developed predictive algorithms to identify anomalous voter registration submissions and flag duplicate or fraudulent credentials.
- **Civic Digital Infrastructure:** Built end-to-end features supporting digital voter registration, verification protocols, candidate nomination submissions, and real-time civic notifications.
- **Secure System Design:** Formulated role-based access control and encrypted data communication channels to safeguard sensitive voter records.
