**Publication:** IEEE ICTEST 2025  
**Tech Stack:** Python, Machine Learning, Mobile Systems, REST APIs, Security  

### Key Capabilities

- **Voter Fraud Detection:** Incorporated an algorithmic verification layer to identify anomalous registration patterns, duplicate identity submissions, and credential tampering.
- **Automated Civic Workflows:** Enabled seamless citizen voter registration, polling station locator, candidate manifesto access, and nomination submission tracking.
- **High-Integrity Infrastructure:** Designed with tamper-resistant logs and strict identity confidentiality.
