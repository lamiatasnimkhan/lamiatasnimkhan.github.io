### Highlights

Niramoy was designed to bridge the urban-rural nursing disparity in Bangladesh by connecting home-bound patients with certified professional healthcare attendants:
- **Real-Time Nurse Dispatch:** Built an on-demand booking pipeline with real-time nurse availability status, geolocated matching, and appointment confirmation.
- **Patient Profile & Vitals Log:** Implemented secure record storage on Firebase for logging blood pressure, glucose, and patient history.
- **Authentication & Security:** Deployed Firebase Phone and Email Auth with granular role-based security rules separating patients and medical providers.
