# Biography

I am a Computer Science and Engineering graduate from **Chittagong University of Engineering and Technology (CUET)**, Bangladesh (B.Sc. in CSE, CGPA: **3.79 / 4.00**).

Currently, I work as a **Research Assistant** at **Elite Research Lab** under the mentorship of [Md. Kishor Morol](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=pjn3jg4AAAAJ&hl=en) (CEO, Elite Research Lab; AI Safety Researcher, Meta; Incoming Researcher, Anthropic), investigating sequential vision-language conversational benchmarks, computational stylometry for AI-generated deceptive text, and formal causal identifiability of memory failures in LLM-based autonomous agents. I am also working as a **Junior Software Engineer** at **OMUKK** (joined October 2026).

While studying at CUET, I worked as an undergraduate researcher in the **CUET Natural Language Processing (NLP) Lab** supervised by [Prof. Dr. Mohammed Moshiul Hoque](https://scholar.google.com/citations?hl=en&user=srYxYhcAAAAJ), developing parameter-efficient and small instruction-tuned large language models for constrained multilingual semantic evaluation, which resulted in two published papers at SemEval-2026. Separately, as part of an international shared-task effort (team CUET_320), I co-authored the multimodal framework that achieved **1st Place Globally** in the **LT-EDI@LDK 2025** shared task on multimodal misogyny meme detection in Chinese social media.

My undergraduate thesis, *"Multimodal AI for Early Detection of Parkinson's Disease,"* was advised by [Prof. AHM Ashfak Habib](https://scholar.google.com/citations?hl=en&user=0g6uXIYAAAAJ) (Dept. of CSE, CUET), investigating multimodal deep learning approaches for early Parkinson's disease detection using heterogeneous clinical and biomedical data, with SHAP and Grad-CAM for model interpretability.

# Research Interests

- **Multimodal Learning & Vision-Language Models (VLMs):** Cross-modal representation alignment, contextual reasoning in sequential multimodal narratives (comics/visual dialogue), and sociolinguistic meme interpretation.
- **Large Language Models & Constrained Reasoning:** Instruction-tuned LLMs, few-shot prompt formulation, psycholinguistic marker identification, and constrained multilingual text generation.
- **AI Agents & Memory Dynamics:** Diagnosing failure modes across extraction, update, retrieval, and consolidation stages in long-horizon LLM agents using causal validation.
- **Explainable Medical AI:** Multimodal deep learning for neurodegenerative disease diagnosis (Parkinson's), biomarker attribution, SHAP, and Grad-CAM interpretability.
- **Low-Resource & Regional NLP (Bengali):** Benchmark dataset creation, stylometric modeling of synthetic deceptive text, and cross-cultural affective computing.
