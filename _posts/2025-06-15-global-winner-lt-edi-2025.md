Our research team (**CUET_320**) achieved **1st Place Globally** in the **LT-EDI@LDK 2025 International Shared Task on Multimodal Misogyny Meme Detection in Chinese Social Media**, held at the 5th Conference on Language, Data and Knowledge (LDK 2025).

We designed an end-to-end multimodal deep learning pipeline fusing visual semantic embeddings with multilingual language model representations, outperforming all competing international teams on macro F1-score.
